Privacy Policy

Pulsewing for Android · version of October 15, 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you play Pulsewing (the "game"), why, and how you can control it. The game is developed by CoderHans ("we"). On first launch the game asks you to accept this policy; by tapping "Accept" you consent to the data processing described below, including for showing ads.

1. What the game stores on your device

Game progress — crystals, records, hangar upgrades, sound and language settings, whether you finished the tutorial and accepted this policy — is stored only on your device. We do not send it to our servers; the game has no accounts or sign-up. Uninstalling the game deletes this progress.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from the VK Ads network (myTarget SDK, VK LLC): optional rewarded videos (second chance, double crystals) and interstitial ads between runs, limited by the game's own frequency rules. To select and show ads, count impressions and prevent fraud, the ad SDK may receive from your device:

  • the device advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), which you can reset or limit in Android settings;
  • technical device and system details: model, Android version, language, time zone, mobile carrier, connection type;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (city, country);
  • information about ad impressions and clicks.

This data is received and processed by VK LLC under its own rules; we do not collect or store it ourselves. Ads may be personalized, i.e. chosen based on interests. See the VK Ads documents at ads.vk.com for details.

3. App permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load and show ads. The game works offline; there are simply no ads or ad rewards.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — required by the ad SDK, see section 2.

The game does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location.

4. Purchases

There are no real-money purchases in the game. Crystals are earned only by playing.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child used the game and their data was processed without parental consent, contact us and we will help.

6. Your rights and withdrawing consent

  • To reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization: Android Settings → Google → Ads (menu names vary by phone).
  • To withdraw consent completely: uninstall the game — all its data is deleted and the ad SDK stops receiving information.
  • For questions, information about data processing, or to withdraw consent: coderhans@yandex.ru.

Data is processed on the basis of your consent in accordance with Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

7. Changes

If the data or terms change, we will update this page and its date. For significant changes the game will show the policy again at launch and ask for consent.

8. Contact
